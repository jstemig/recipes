1/2 c sugar
4-5 T butter
1/2 c water
3# apples, peeled (golden delicious or similar) (not sure on total amount)
  Cut enough into quarters to fill the bottom of the skillet
  Cut remaining into smaller slices

pastry
1 c flour
4 T butter
pinch salt
pinch sugar
1 T water

Make carmel: 
Mix sugar, butter & water.  
Boil till it starts to brown around edges.

Into the boiling carmel, add the larger apple chunks neatly, enough to cover the bottom of the skillet
Add remaining smaller slices on top.
Add 1/4 c water and cover.

Cook apples till they are getting soft.  Then, uncover and simmer till set up and not watery.  

Pastry
Process flour, butter, sugar and salt in food processor till gravely.  Then add 1-2 T water, and blend until it can be gathered together.  Rest dough.

Roll out dough to match the size of the skillet.  Flop dough on top of cooking apples.  

Bake @ 350 till crust is golden brown.  

Let cool.  Invert pan onto plate.  If it's stuck, warm it on the stove till the carmel loosens.  

Serve with ice cream, whipped cream, english cream, etc.
