600 g cherry tomatoes, halved 
200 g rosemary focaccia, torn
16 oz cooked white beans
2 T red wine vinegar
400 g chipolata sausage 

Oven to 350

Combine tomatoes, bread, beans, vinegar and a drizzle of olive oil, pour into baking dish.
Tuck in the sausages in and on the mix in the pan.

Bake 45-50 mins till sausages are golden brown.
