Sausage
Jacque Pepin & Julia Child 

base:
1 1/2 lbs ground pork shoulder 
2 1/2 t table salt 
3/4 t ground black pepper 
1 clove garlic, finely chopped 
1/2 t sugar 
pinch saltpeter 

country-style add:
2-3 T chopped walnuts (or pecans or pistachios)
3 T red wine 


fancy-style add:
1 julienned black truffle
2 T pinenuts
3 T white wine 

Mix

Roll up in parchment, then tinfoil

Cure for 3 days in fridge 

Poach in water in wrapper 35-40 mins
