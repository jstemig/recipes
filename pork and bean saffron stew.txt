1/4 onion, chopped
4-5 leaves fresh sage, chopped
1 clove garlic, chopped
1 lb ground pork 
1 28 oz can whole peeled tomatoes, chopped
pinch of saffron
1 can chick peas

Sweat onions and sage till soft.  Add garlic, then pork.  
Brown pork.  Add tomatoes and saffron.  

Bring to boil, reduce to simmer, cook 1 hour.  Add chickpeas, cook another 30-60 mins.

Finish with fresh basil & good evoo.
