1 kg russet potato
4 strips bacon, chopped
1 garlic clove, thinly sliced
1/2 c cream
200 g green peas, frozen

Microwave potatos 25 minutes.  Scoop out guts.  Combine w/ 40-80 g flour to make a dough.  

Roll out dough to snake, cut into 1 inch pieces, and squash with finger or fork.  

Crisp up the bacon in a large skillet.  Add garlic.  Add peas, and mash them up a bit with a spoon. 

Cook gnocchi in water - they are done when they float.  Add gnocchi to the saute pan with the bacon mix.  

Add cream and simmer 1-2 mins to combine.
